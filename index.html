Academic Research & Publications
World Bank Collaboration
Economic Impact of Hurricanes on Local Economies
Mastercard Economics Institute · with Leora Klapper, Mingxuan Fan & Sumit Agarwal

Leveraged Mastercard's high-frequency transaction data to study how natural disasters affect local economies and the pace of recovery across sectors. Key findings: spending declines ~60% on the day of landfall; recovery takes ~2 weeks; restaurants and retail face the sharpest declines; merchants with stronger online presence show greater resilience.

The Ripple Effects of the ICC Cricket World Cup on the Indian Economy
Mastercard Economics Institute · Cricketonomics Series

Quantified the economic impact of cricket tournaments on India using hyperlocal transaction data. Hotels and restaurants in host cities such as Ahmedabad and Lucknow saw spend boosts of up to 36% around match days compared to equivalent non-game days.
